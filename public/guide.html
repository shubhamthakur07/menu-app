2. Customer Kse Use Karega? (Customer Flow)

Step 1: Table QR Scan Karna

Customer apne phone ka camera open karke table standee par lage QR code ko scan karta hai. Koi app download nahi karni padti, direct Chrome/Safari me menu khul jata hai.

Step 2: Menu Dekhna & Items Add Karna

Customer photos, veg/non-veg badge, aur prices dekhkar + button dabakar dishes cart me add karta hai.

Step 3: Checkout & Special Instructions

Customer apna Naam, Mobile Number aur cooking note dalta hai (jaise: "less spicy, send hot"). Yeh note seedha chef ke pass jata hai.

Step 4: Live Status & Food Tracking

Order place hote hi customer ko live status dikhta hai: Received ➜ In Kitchen (Cooking) ➜ Food Ready ➜ Served. Jab chef status badalta hai toh customer ke phone par sound alert aata hai!

Step 5: Aur Items Mangwana (+ Order More)

Agar khana khate waqt extra roti ya cold drink mangwani ho, toh customer "+ Order More Items" par tap karta hai. Uski table aur details automatically bhari rehti hain aur bill update ho jata hai.

3. Hotel Owner & Staff Kse Use Karega? (Dashboard Guide)

1. Live Orders Screen

Counter par tablet ya laptop open rakhein. Naya order aane par bell bajegi aur card appear hoga. Page reload karne ki zarurat nahi hai.

2. Kitchen Status Buttons

• In Kitchen: Chef ne khana banana shuru kiya.
• Food Ready: Dishes tayar ho chuki hain.
• Served: Waiter ne table par deliver kar diya.

3. Mark Paid Button

Jab customer counter par cash/card deta hai, owner Mark Paid dabata hai jisse customer ka bill settle ho jata hai.

4. Floor & Table Management

Har table ka live status dikhta hai (Green = Free, Red = Occupied). Owner yahin se QR Code download karke print kar sakta hai.

5. Home Delivery Toggle

Settings me jakar owner jab chahe Home Delivery ko ON ya OFF kar sakta hai.

6. Menu Dishes & Prices

Kabhi bhi naya dish add karein, photo badlein, ya agar koi item khatam ho gaya ho toh use 1-click me out of stock mark karein.

Owner ke liye Pro-Tip:

Restaurant counter par ek Android tablet ya laptop laga kar uski volume full rakhein aur dashboard me "Enable Alerts" par click kar dein. Har naye order par ghanti bajegi aur koi order miss nahi hoga!

4. Frequently Asked Questions (Aam Sawal)
Q: Kya customer ko Play Store se app download karni padegi?

Nahi! Customer sirf camera se QR code scan karega aur direct Google Chrome ya Safari me khul jayega.

Q: Agar do log Table 1 par ek sath order de rahe hain toh kya hoga?

System active table ko lock kar leta hai. Agar koi aur Table 1 par order dene ki koshish karega toh use notification aayega ki yeh table abhi occupied hai, free table select karein.

Q: UPI se payment ka paisa kahan jata hai?

Owner Settings me jo apna UPI ID dalta hai, customer ka paisa direct usi bank account me jata hai. Koi commission ya beech ka platform nahi hota.
